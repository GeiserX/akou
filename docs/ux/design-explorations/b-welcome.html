First-time setup

Welcome to akou

akou records your calls on this Mac, writes the transcript while people talk, and pins each note to the moment you took it. There are three things to set up. Only the first one is needed before your first recording.

1

Speech models

Required 2.9 GB

These models turn call audio into text and tell the voices apart. They run on this Mac, so recording works offline and no audio is uploaded anywhere.

Speech recognizerTurns what is said into text as it happens
2,480 MB
Speaker labellerTells voices apart so the transcript shows who spoke
512 MB
Kept in ~/Library/Application Support/akou/models. This one download is all akou fetches. Nothing else leaves this computer.
One download of 2,992 MB. You can keep working while it runs.
2

Microphone and system audio

Later

The microphone hears you. System audio hears the other side of the call. macOS asks for both the first time you press Record, so there is nothing to do now.

Microphone · asked on first recording System audio · asked on first recording
3

Your agent

Optional

Connect an AI agent you already use to answer questions about a call and write enhanced notes. Without one, akou still records, transcribes and keeps your notes.

You can change any of this later in Settings.
