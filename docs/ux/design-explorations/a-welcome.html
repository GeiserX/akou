Welcome to akou

akou records your calls on this Mac, writes the transcript as people talk, and keeps your notes on the same timeline.

  1. 1

    Download the speech models

    One download of 2.9 GB. The models stay on this Mac and transcribe offline, so your audio never leaves the computer.

    2.9 GB · about 5 min on a fast connection
