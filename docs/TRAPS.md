# Traps

A trap is a failure that already bit us once and is now written down so it cannot bite twice. This file lists every trap from the predecessors (hark, hark-viewer, the call skill) and from the akou spikes that still applies to akou's design, rewritten as an invariant or a regression test in the form "given X, akou must Y". Ids in brackets point into the design inventory (`T<reader>.<n>`, see [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md)) or say `spike`. Each entry becomes a named test, and the milestone in parentheses says when. When the test cannot be a Bun test named after the trap's id, the entry says so with `Test (release checklist):`, `Test (on hardware):`, `Test (nightly):` or `Test (not written yet):`. `scripts/trap-coverage.ts` lists those in CI and fails every other reached trap that has an id but no test with that id in its name that ran and asserted, and every reached entry with no id at all.

Traps that belong to the user's own downstream pipelines (archive formats, word-list harvesting, search indexes, merging with a second recording source) are not listed: T3.16 to T3.22, T3.28, T3.44 and T3.45. akou hands those pipelines clean material and owns none of them. Traps whose feature akou does not have are listed at the end under "Retired with the feature", so nobody mistakes a retired trap for a forgotten one.

## Capture

- **A first-mic bound counted from before the start's own clock** [T0.16, CI flake] (M1). Given the Rust helper in file mode on a Windows runner, the `[T0.16]` scenario bounded the first mic packet at one second plus `startMs` after the start request. `startMs` counts from the call's `begin`, but the call's folder, lock and `call.created` are written synchronously before it. On a fresh runner whose disk stalled, that took about 1 s with the event loop held (no timer turn for 1,071 ms), the helper's open took 380 ms more, and the mic came 115 ms after `capturing`: a correct helper failed by 57 to 173 ms, 4 times in 279 CI runs from 2026-10-10 to 2026-10-30, every time on `helper (windows-latest)`. Repeated on a warm runner it never failed (199 runs); on 20 fresh runners, each running the step once after the build, it failed once, the same way. The time rule of T0.16 counts from the moment the helper said `capturing`, when its timeline starts, and the budget is the trap's own first second: the mic's first packet must reach the app within 1 s of `capturing`. Test: `firstMicLateBroken` in `[T0.16] silent from the start`; in `tests/capture-traps.test.ts`, a stall of 1.5 s injected while the folder is written, where the old bound fails and the new one passes, and the positive control, a fake helper that holds the mic 1.5 s (`--mic-held-until`), whose first packet is still at 0 s and fails the check.
- **Dead call side hidden behind a live mic** [T0.2, T1.29] (M1). Given the OS reports output running and the call stream has delivered exact zeros for 10 s, or, after delivering, no buffers at all for 1 s, akou must open a probe, rebuild the call stream if the probe hears audio, back off 10/30/60 s then every minute, stop after 5 rebuilds per part, and write a `health {state: dead}` event and a red banner. Given a zero run with output not running, akou must not rebuild; given buffers of zeros, akou must not probe before 10 s. Test: a fake helper and the Rust helper with `call-dead-at` (no buffers: dead within 2 s) and `call-zeros-at` (zeros: dead after 10 s); a scripted tap whose probe answers the moment the stream's own audio returns, ahead of that audio's slot (no rebuild), with the positive control of the same probe while the stream stays dead (rebuilt); a real-device test with a debug kill switch that is compiled out of release builds, with a test proving the switch is absent.
- **A tap that starts with the call** [G4 quiet-tap run] (M1). Given the call side delivered nothing while nothing played and the OS reports output running a moment before the tap's first buffer, akou must not probe or rebuild the tap, and the first word must be whole and in its place from its first sample. Given a rebuilt call stream on a clock a few milliseconds from the old one, akou must place it by its own timestamps at once, not slew it back over tens of seconds. Test: a scripted tap silent for 12 s whose output is reported running 300 ms early (no `dead`, no rebuild, onset within 2 ms, the first 20 ms at full level); a rebuilt stream 33 ms off, placed within 2 ms; aligner tests with a restart, a stale buffer after it, and a late first buffer.
- **A hung OS teardown** [T0.9] (M0). Given the helper's stop does not return within 5 s, akou must kill it, finalize the log, mark `part.ended {reason: killed}`, and accept a new start immediately in a fresh helper. Test: a debug-only "hang on teardown" switch; measure that the next `POST /calls` answers 201 within 3 s.
- **Unbounded queue when one source stops** [T0.15] (M0). Given one source delivers nothing for 10 minutes while the other keeps going, akou must keep both channels frame-aligned with zeros on the silent side, use bounded memory, and write a file whose two channels have the same length. Test: the packet gap test from the capture spike, extended to 10 minutes.
- **A tap silent from the start blocks the mic** [T0.16, T1.33] (M0). Given nothing plays on the computer for the first minutes of a call, akou must still deliver mic frames from the first second, and the first `seg` on the mic channel must appear. Test: start capture with the output device idle for 2 minutes while speaking into a virtual mic.
- **Permission grant keyed by path** [T0.18] (M0, G3). Given a Developer ID signed update that changes the binary, akou must keep its microphone and system-audio grants. Test (release checklist): with the grants given to the current signed release, install the next signed release over it, record 10 s, assert the call channel is not all zeros and that no prompt appeared.
- **Permission attribution to the wrong process** [spike, T0.19 closed] (M0). Given the helper is spawned by the app, the grant prompt must name akou and the grant must attach to the app bundle, never to a terminal. Test on hardware; if it fails, build the capture crate as the in-process addon.
- **Backend selection without a fallback** [T0.27]. Given the design, akou must never depend on ScreenCaptureKit for audio. Test: a grep that `ScreenCaptureKit` does not appear in the capture crate.
- **Health verdicts on the wrong format** [T0.28, T1.31, T0.7]. Given any device format (16, 24, 32-bit integer or float, interleaved or not), akou must convert to float at the edge and compute every health verdict on converted data with the maximum over all channels. Given a rebuild, akou must re-derive and re-announce the health capability. Test: property tests over every input format in the converter; a rebuild followed by a `health` event.
- **Probe click in Bluetooth headphones** [T0.29] (M1). Given a Bluetooth headset, building the probe device must not produce an audible click. Test (release checklist): a human listening check in the M1 hardware release checklist (it needs the Rust probe device, which exists only once the helper is built; [DESIGN risk F12](DESIGN.md#12-risks-and-falsifiers)). If it clicks, the probe runs only on non-Bluetooth routes and the banner says "no lines for 90 s" instead.
- **Own audio in the call channel** [spike] (M1). Given the window plays audio, that audio must not appear in the call channel on macOS or Windows. On macOS akou must exclude the WebKit GPU helper process ("akou Graphics and Media"), not the app's process id, and re-resolve the exclusion on every rebuild. On Linux the window must not play audio while recording. Test: play a tone from the window during capture; assert the right channel is silent.
- **A device that only runs at one rate** [T4.21] (M1). Given a device whose only supported rate differs from the default, akou must open it at its native rate and resample. Test: Rust tests in the capture crate: a 1 kHz tone resampled from 44.1 to 48 kHz stays at 1 kHz (`resample.rs`), and sources at 44.1 and 16 kHz land on the timeline at their level (`aligner.rs`). No test yet drives a fake device that offers only one rate.
- **Room noise above the silence floor** [T4.23]. Given a mic idling at -48 dBFS, akou must still segment speech, because it uses Silero VAD only and no amplitude threshold. Test (not written yet): the VAD fixtures with -48 dBFS pink noise mixed in.
- **Capture priority under load** [T3.8] (M1). Given a fully loaded CPU, the audio file must have no gaps; the transcript may lag and must say so. Test: the soak with a CPU burner at 100 % on every core; assert the file duration matches wall time within 100 ms and an `asr.lag` event appears.
- **Wake from sleep** [spike, ElectroBun #550] (M1). Given the machine sleeps mid-call, akou must hold a keep-awake assertion while recording, and if it sleeps anyway, write a `gap` event on wake, keep wall times correct, and keep the recording running even if the window froze. Test: a monotonic-clock jump injected into a fake helper.
- **Aggregate device leaks** [I0.14]. Given a killed helper, no `akou-capture` or `akou-probe` aggregate device may remain. Test (not written yet): enumerate devices after a kill. Today the aggregate is private, so it goes away with the helper's process.
- **Drift between two clocks** [T4.33, F0.28] (M0). Given 60 minutes of recording with a 1.5 kHz ping every 10 s heard by both channels, the left-right offset must stay under 200 ms per hour and gaps under 20 ms. Test (on hardware): `scripts/drift-test.ts` on real devices. On record since 2026-10-08 with both channels on the host clock (ROADMAP G4: 0.01 ms per hour); two independent device clocks are not measured, a known limitation ([M0 results](gates/M0-results.md#g4-the-criterion-as-of-2026-10-10)).
- **A dictation smoke that stops before the first sample** [DC-N5 smoke, CI flake] (M1). Given the real key tap on a macOS runner with `--warm off`, the smoke posted a tap and sent `stop` as soon as the posts were done, about one second after the press. A session waits for its first real sample before `session.started` (DC-N4's readiness gate), and a session with no sample yet ends with nothing said when `stop` comes, by design. On one fresh runner the cold Core Audio stream took longer than that second, so the log read `press`, `mic open:true`, `mic open:false`, `stopped` and the check said "no session started"; a rerun passed. Across 210 presses on 10 fresh runners the stream delivered 104 to 448 ms after it opened and the old order missed none, so the miss is rare (the only one in the last 1,000 CI runs) but real. The smoke must wait for `session.started`, up to 10 s, before it stops. Test: the step's control runs the simulate build's RightCommand tap (key-down at 100 ms opens the mic, key-up at 150 ms starts the session) with a mic 1.5 s slow to deliver. A stop 0.3 s after the mic opens, so after the key-up and before the first sample (the CI failure's order), must start no session; the same tap with the wait must start one; and the same early stop with no mic delay must start one, which proves the early stop came after the key-up. A stop before the key-up starts no session whatever the mic does, so a control that skips that last check passes with no slow mic at all, which the first version of this control did. Every run first waits for the tap's `mic open:true`, so none can pass with no press. On a 20-run loop the control passed 20 of 20; with the slow-mic run's delay set to 0 it failed 20 of 20, and with the 0.3 s sleep removed it failed 20 of 20.

## Transcription

- **Short spans lose their words** [T1.9, T4.19] (M1). Given a 0.2 s "Yes." on any path (live, final, single file), akou must pad it to 0.5 s with trailing zeros in one shared function and transcribe it. The 0.3 s floor behind this came from hark's engines and is unmeasured on sherpa-onnx. The streaming-zipformer evidence about padding is contradictory and must not be carried to Parakeet: one spike test said leading padding loses the first words, and the vocabulary spike found the opposite, that a fresh stream of that model drops the first words until it has had 2 to 8 s of leading audio. Test: the 0.2 s fixture through each path, run against Parakeet through sherpa-onnx, with leading padding, trailing padding and none as three conditions, and the result recorded.
- **A chunk fed twice** [T1.11]. Given a non-float source, akou must feed every frame exactly once. Test: frame-count property tests in the converter.
- **Quadratic token mapping** [T1.19]. Given a 3-hour call, per-question and per-segment work must stay bounded. Test: pack build and segment commit timings on a 3-hour synthetic log stay flat.
- **Stop during a slow start marked failed** [T1.26] (M1). Given a stop before `capturing`, akou must cancel the part with `reason: cancelled`, never "wedged" or failed. No timeout may begin before the helper is spawned, and the start budget ([DESIGN 2.4](DESIGN.md#24-the-helper-protocol-akou-capture1): 3 s warm, 10 s on the first cold tap) is the only deadline. Tests: (a) stop 200 ms after start with a fake cold-tap helper that delays `capturing` by 3 s, inside the budget; (b) a fake helper that delays `capturing` past the budget, and the start fails cleanly with `503 capture_failed {stage: "open"}` and a `call.failed` event.
- **Spanish worse on the streaming model** [T1.44]. Given a Spanish fixture, the live path must not use a streaming model that is worse than the segmented one. Test (nightly): WER per language with a floor.
- **Only channel 0 read** [T2.7, T4.17] (M1). Given a file with speech only on the right channel, the final pass must produce call lines. Given the call channel has energy and the final pass produced no text, `final.done` must carry a warning. Test: a right-only fixture.
- **VAD gating loses words** [spike] (M1). Given a short word after 0.8 s of silence that Silero VAD misses in a continuous stream, the final pass must still transcribe it, because VAD only proposes cut points and every second of audio above silence is decoded. Test: the spike fixture where VAD drops "Thanks."; assert it appears in the final layer.
- **Invented sentence on silence** [T4.37]. Given a file, or a stretch of one, with no speech (room noise, music, silence), a model writes a sentence anyway: Qwen3-ASR answers noise with "thank you for watching" or loops on one word ("the the the ..."), as Qwen3, Moonshine and Cohere did before sherpa-onnx fixed them in 1.13.7 and 1.13.8. Since sherpa-onnx 2.0, Parakeet's final pass computes its features with a gentler log floor: quiet speech that came out empty now decodes, and faint noise at -50 to -70 dBFS handed to it with no VAD in front comes out as whole invented sentences, mostly Spanish. Bazarr sends whole films, so that text becomes a wrong subtitle. Every job, on every preset, must run the VAD before any model loads; a file with no speech must end `done` with `text: ""`, `segments: []` and `note: no_speech`, with no model loaded; no engine may be handed a piece in which the VAD heard no speech; and a decoded piece that says a phrase of one or two words eight times or more in a row, or of three or four words five times or more, must keep the phrase once and the words after it, in its text and its words alike. Speech repeats short phrases ("No, no, no, no, no!", "Go! Go! Go!"), so a lower bar or a cut that drops the rest of the line loses real dialogue; Han text has no spaces and its sentence mark is a token of its own, so a loop such as 谢谢观看。 is matched by character with punctuation passed over. Each piece left undecoded and each cut is a `debug` line with times and counts, never the text. Tests: `tests/silence-guards.test.ts` `[T4.37]` (noise through each preset's pass, a positive control in which an energy gate hands the same noise to the engines and they invent, the real Silero VAD on white and pink noise at -30 dBFS, and at -40 and -50 dBFS, where the peaks clear the energy gate and only the VAD stops it, and on the generated two-voice speech fixture in noise, where its model is on disk, the loop guard, with film lines that repeat a word kept whole and a Han loop cut in its text and its words), and through the API in `jobs.e2e`, `best-preset.e2e`, `fusion-preset.e2e` and `bazarr.e2e`.
- **Speaker labels add words** [akou-5an.100] (M1). Given the same audio, a job with `diarize` on must give the words of the job with it off, labelled, and nothing else. A diarizer's turn edge sits a frame or two off the pause, and a cut there left the tail of a word, or a sliver of the pause, as a piece of its own. An engine decodes such a sliver into "Yeah." or "Okay." (Parakeet on 0.2.1's `fast`, where it was reported, and Qwen on `best`), helped by `prepareSpan`, which gains the sliver by up to 20 dB and pads it to 0.5 s; a cut inside a word gave a fragment. Each edge must move to the quietest window of the nearest pause within 0.5 s (the VAD's own cut in that pause), but never more than under half its own turn, so a short reply with no pause before it keeps its own piece and label; and a piece in which the VAD heard no speech must never be decoded, on any engine. Tests: `tests/job-pass.test.ts` `[akou-5an.100]`, with a positive control that cuts at the edges as they stand and gains words, and a 0.25 s reply that must stay its own line.
- **Empty audio still loads models** [T4.18]. Given a part with no energy, the final pass must skip it before loading any model. Test: a silent part; assert model load is not called.
- **Free memory read as free pages** [T4.38, #378]. Given macOS keeps nearly every page busy caching files, `os.freemem()` counts only the free ones: 0.19 GB on a 24 GB Mac with 9 GB to give, so a threshold on it never loads anything. A decision on available memory must read the kernel's available figure on macOS (`kern.memorystatus_level`) and `MemAvailable` on Linux. Test: `tests/start-warm.test.ts`, both parsers and this machine's figure against its own source.
- **Models loaded twice** [T4.18, F1.7]. Given two channels and several parts, each model must be loaded once per app run. Test: a load counter.
- **A first-run download that fills the disk** [MD-1] (M1). Given the window's first-run Download fetches every model at once, it must check the volume's free space first, as one model's download does: the missing bytes plus 1 GB to spare. A pull that skipped the check wrote until `ENOSPC`, left the volume at zero bytes free, and the next call could not write its audio or its log. Test: `tests/first-run.test.ts`, a volume that reports 1 byte free (the pull fails naming the bytes free, and the model server gets no request), with room on the volume as the positive control.
- **Undefined order of simultaneous lines** [T1.42]. Given two lines with the same `w0`, every reader must order mic before call, then by `seq`. Test: a unit test on the sort key.
- **Harmless engine noise on stderr** [T1.45]. Given a model prints warnings, they must go to the app log, never to the user. Test: stderr capture in the Worker.
- **Relabel wastes a transcription** [T4.32]. Given a re-cluster request, akou must not transcribe again. Test: `akou name --merge` completes without a recognizer call.
- **A whole-chunk stream Nemotron refuses** [spike] (M1). parakeet-rs 0.3.8 hands ONNX Runtime a column-major array ("Array has a non-contiguous layout") when a whole stream, or the remainder a live flush decides, is one chunk whose feature count (`samples / 160 + 1`) is a multiple of 8: a 250 s live run died on its closing flush, and a final pass over a call of up to 30.4 s fails one time in eight. Given any stream or remainder length, `akou-diarize` must decide it, never passing the padding it adds on to a caller. Tests: `whole_chunk_pad` over every length to 40 000 samples and the two-timeline test in `native/akou-diarize/src/lib.rs`, and the lengths that failed (2400 and 2401 samples) run against the real model before the fix was accepted. parakeet-rs 0.4.0 passes the chunk in the standard layout, so the refusal is gone upstream; the padding stays until a run against the real model shows every length decides without it.
- **A model download that fails without a word** [SV-M3, SV-U6] (M1). Given a single model's download gives up after its retries, or is refused at once, the row used to fall back to a plain Download as if nothing had been tried: the cause reached only the log and the jobs waiting on it. The store must keep why each model's last download gave up, `GET /models` must carry it as the row's `error` while the model is missing, and the Models page and the live panel's Add a model must say "The download stopped: <reason>." with Try again. A new download clears it, and a Cancel is on purpose, so it leaves none. The model's files arriving or going by another door (an import, the desktop's download, a delete, the sweep) clear it too, or a later missing row would show a stop that did not happen this time. It lives in memory, so a restart forgets it. Tests: a store test that fails a download for good, then pulls and cancels; a store test that fails it, brings the files in by another door, then deletes or evicts them; window tests with a failed row on both surfaces.
- **A resume refused for bytes already on disk** [SV-M2] (M1). Given a download that was cancelled or gave up leaves its partial `<file>.part` for the next pull, the size cap and the free-space check counted every unfinished file at its full size: a resume that needed 0.4 GB of a 2.4 GB model was refused on a volume with 1.5 GB free, and the partial bytes counted twice against `server.models_max_gb`. With no download or copy running, a file's need must be its size less its partial file (none when the partial file is larger than the file, which the download starts over). Test: a store test with a partial file on disk, at the exact free space and cap, with the byte before each as the control.
- **A live line that starts inside the line before it** [LV-1] (M1). Given `asr.segmentPause` under 0.7 s on the Parakeet live path, a line closed `postRoll` (0.2 s) after its last speech, and the next one opened `preRoll` (0.5 s) before its first, so the two overlapped by 0.7 s minus the pause: 0.4 s at a 0.3 s pause, and the next line could start with the end of the last word of the line before. The next line must never start before the last closed line ended, on the same channel and part. Test: `tests/asr-live.test.ts` `[LV-1]`, a 0.3 s pause (the second line starts where the first ended) with the default pause as the control (its pre-roll is whole).
- **A live decode that throws shifts the rest of the part** [LV-2] (M1). Given a one-off native error in a Parakeet live decode, the error left the channel's unfinished VAD window where it was before the audio message, so every later line on that channel was cut from audio one message too early, and the line was lost with only an app-log error. A decode that throws must lose only its own line, with a warning that names its span (the final pass decodes that audio again), and the channel's timeline must stay with its audio whatever a decode throws, a provisional re-decode included. Test: `tests/asr-live.test.ts` `[LV-2]`, a recognizer that throws once, on a closing decode and on a provisional one: the other lines have the times of a run with no error.
- **A failing final pass that holds the start's catch-up** [FN-1] (M1). Given a final pass that fails by running out of its budget (Qwen on a machine with no GPU and a long call), the start's catch-up runs it again at every start, for its whole budget, and every call that ended later waited behind it. A call whose last pass failed must run after every call that has not had a pass, and must still run, so audio that becomes readable later still gets its final layer. Test: `tests/asr-final-catchup.e2e.test.ts` `[FN-1]`, a failed call that the start meets first and a call with no pass: the call with no pass starts first and both end `done`.

## Event log and clocks

- **Offsets shown as times of day** [T3.9] (M1). Given any rendering for a person or a model, akou must show local wall-clock time and never a bare `mm:ss` offset. Test: a renderer test that greps every pack, export and window row for `^\d\d:\d\d$`-style offsets and fails on any.
- **Clock drifts by paused time** [T2.52] (M1). Given a 5-minute pause, a line after it must carry the correct wall time. Test: a fake helper with a pause; assert `w0`.
- **Failed start leaves an orphan folder** [T2.49] (M1). Given a start that never captured, akou must write `call.failed`, keep the folder and any audio, list it under `--failed`, and never present it as live. Test: a fake helper exiting 77.
- **Restart splits one call into several folders** [T3.2, T2.48] (M1). Given any number of restarts, one call must be one folder, one log, one clock. Test: three restarts; `akou show` lists three parts in one call.
- **Speaker numbers restart per part** [T2.48, T3.10] (M1). Given a restart, cluster ids must continue (centroids persisted), and names given before the restart must still render after it. Test: name `c2` in part 1, restart, assert a `c2` segment in part 2 renders the name. With Nemotron live labels the diarizer's stream must not be reset at a part end, and a stream that starts over (a new app run, a new Worker, a restarted helper) must take labels back by centroid, never renumber a known voice as a new one nor give a known label to a new voice. Tests: `tests/asr-stream.test.ts`, both `[T2.48]` cases, each with a positive control.
- **A wrong speaker merge with no way back** [judging] (M1). Given a `speaker.merge`, `speaker.unmerge` must restore the two ids for later segments and the window must offer it. Test: merge, unmerge, assert rendering.
- **`current` points at a finished call** [T3.14] (M1). Given no live call, `live` must return 404 with `last` in the body, every pack must start with `LIVE` or `ENDED at HH:MM`, and `last` must be refused on live controls. Test: ask after stop; assert 404 and the status line; assert `POST /calls/last/stop` answers 400.
- **Whole transcript re-read per question** [T3.12] (M1). Given a 3-hour call, an MCP `akou_context` call must return at most the requested budget (default 6k tokens) regardless of length. Test: a synthetic 3-hour log; assert token count.
- **Disk reads give part 1 only** [T3.13] (M1). Given the on-disk layout, there must be no per-part transcript file that an agent could read by mistake. Test: a directory listing assertion after a multi-part call.
- **Names lost after the agent's context is compacted** [T3.10] (M1). Given a `speaker.name` written by an agent, a new agent session's first `akou_context` must contain it in the roster. Test: name, then call context from a fresh client.
- **Answers from uncorrected recognition** [T3.11] (M1). Given a vocabulary entry `Kubernetes` with heard forms `kubernetis` and `cubernetes`, the pack and the export must show `Kubernetes (heard: "kubernetis")` and the window must show the raw text on hover; the log must keep the raw text. Test: a fixture with the variant.
- **Torn last line** [F2.50]. Given a truncated final line, readers must ignore it and the writer must truncate it at the next open. Test: chop the file mid-line.
- **A follower re-reads the whole log per event** [T1.47] (M1). Given an agent following a 3-hour call (`akou events -f`, `akou tail -f`, `akou wait`), each long poll of `GET /calls/:id/events` must read only the bytes appended since its last read, and a poll at the head must read nothing; one that parsed the whole log again for every new event (twice when a `types` filter drops it) costs work that grows with the square of the call's length on the thread that serves the API. A request older than the cursor, or a log that shrank or was replaced, is read whole, and every answer equals a whole read. Tests: `tests/log-io.test.ts` (200 appends to a 20,000-event log read exactly the appended bytes, with a whole-read positive control; a torn tail the writer truncates; a replaced file) and `tests/events-follow.e2e.test.ts` (a line already read, rewritten in place, is not read again).
- **Two writers** [T4.44] (M1). Given a second app instance on the same folder, it must refuse (`.akou.lock`). Test: two writers.
- **A crashed lock whose pid comes back** [LG-2] (M1). Given a lock left by a crash or a power loss, after the reboot its pid can belong to an unrelated live process (pids start low at every boot, and a process of another user counts as alive). The lock was refused for as long as that process lived, so akou would not start and said only that it was already running. A live holder touches its lock every 10 s, so a lock last touched before this boot (by more than a minute, on the kernel's clock that stamps mtimes) must be taken over whatever its pid. Test: `tests/lock.test.ts` `[LG-2]`, a lock naming a live pid with an mtime an hour old and a boot ten minutes ago is taken, in app and server mode. Positive control: the same lock with a boot two hours ago is refused. The CLI reads the same lock while it launches the app, and must not take such a lock for a launch in progress and wait out its whole budget (`tests/cli-launch.e2e.test.ts` `[LG-2]`, a launch that starts nothing fails fast; the control, the same lock with a boot two hours ago, is waited for). Pid reuse within one boot (fast on Windows) is not covered: that needs the holder's start time in the lock.
- **Layer switch confuses a live reader** [design risk] (M1). Given a reader holding live ids across `final.part.done` and `final.done`, the ids must still resolve and the `best` view must switch per part on `final.part.done`. ## Custom vocabulary

Every number here comes from our vocabulary measurements: a synthetic TTS set and a small real-call set.

- **The raw heard text is never overwritten** [decision] (M1). Given any correction (a `vocab.add`, a post-call pass result, an accepted proposal), the `seg` event's `text` must stay as the recognizer wrote it; corrections are events applied at read time, and a whole-line user edit is a `seg` revision that leaves the first revision in the log. Test: apply every correction path to a fixture log and diff the original events.
- **A vocabulary entry never inserts a term where nothing similar was said** [spike] (M1). Given the control clips (sentences with sound-alike words and no listed term), read-time correction must produce zero insertions, and decode biasing at the default boost with a list under the cap must stay under the insertion ceiling. Measured: boost 3 put a listed word into 2 of 30 real negative clips, boost 4 into 11 of 30, boost 5 into 24 of 30. Test: the nightly vocabulary evaluation with a positive control at boost 5 that must breach the ceiling, which proves the test can fail.
- **A mid-call add applies forward to decoding and backward to reading** [decision] (M1). Given a `vocab.add` written during a call, with beam decoding the next segment must be decoded with the word in its stream's list; under greedy, the default, no list is sent and the add works through reading alone. Either way, every earlier segment's rendered text must show the correction at once, with the raw text untouched. Test: the fold suite with a `vocab.add` after segment 10; assert segment 3 renders corrected and the live Worker's next `createStream` call carries the word.
- **The decode list is long** [spike] (M1). Given more entries than the cap, with beam decoding, akou must truncate to 24 in the documented priority order and warn; under greedy no list is sent and `vocab.used` is empty. Measured: 214 words at boost 3 halved the target hits and inserted 43 unsaid names; 398 words changed 23.5 % of the other words on real clips. Test: a 100-entry workspace file; assert `vocab.used` lists 24.
- **The boost is a slider** [spike] (M1). Given the settings schema, there must be no user-facing global boost; the constant is 1.5 and applies only with beam decoding, per-entry values are capped at 5, and only the user sets one above 1.5, as `decode` in a vocabulary file entry; akou never writes a boost. Test: schema assertion plus a config file with `vocab.boost` refused.
- **The `bpe.vocab` from the upstream recipe** [spike] (M1). Given the Parakeet tokenizer, akou must build `bpe.vocab` from the model's own `tokenizer.json` and verify each listed word tokenizes the same way under sherpa-onnx's rules as under the real tokenizer; a word that differs is dropped from the decode list with a warning. Measured: the recipe's file mis-tokenized 7 of 10 terms and cost 8 hits of 140. Test: the tokenization check over the synthetic term list must report 0 mismatches, and a deliberately wrong score file must report them.
- **Hotwords that silently do nothing** [spike] (M1). Given a hotword that fails to encode, akou must log an error and drop it, never continue on a stderr line. Measured: pre-tokenized words without `modelingUnit` failed 7 of 10 and hits stayed at the unbiased 48 of 140. Test: a word with a piece not in the vocab; assert the error and its absence from `vocab.used`.
- **Hotwords to a non-transducer model kill the process** [spike, source read] (M1). Given a non-transducer recognizer (Moonshine, Whisper or a CTC model; akou builds none on sherpa-onnx today, and the guard covers the next one), akou must never call `createStream` with a list or set `hotwordsFile`, because sherpa-onnx logs "Only transducer models support contextual biasing" and exits. The recording would die with it. Test: a Moonshine model config, as the non-transducer stand-in, built with a non-empty vocabulary; assert the Worker's stream calls carry no argument.
- **A hazardous first token** [spike] (M1). Given a term whose first model token is among the 200 most frequent, `akou vocab check` must set `decode: false`. Measured: one such term caused 13 of 19 false insertions at boost 4 and 40 of 61 at boost 5. Test: the check on that class of term.
- **A heard form that is a real word** [spike] (M1). Given a proposed pair whose heard form is a dictionary word or 3 characters or shorter (`vessel`, `ira`, `r`), file entries must not apply it at read time and the skill must propose, never add. Only a call-scoped `vocab.add` may carry such a pair. Test: a fixture log containing the real word; assert it renders unchanged.
- **File vocabulary that silently corrects nothing** [decision] (M1). Read-time correction asks a dictionary before it applies a file pair, an accepted proposal or a fuzzy match (speaker names included), and with no dictionary it applies none of them: only call-scoped `vocab.add` pairs work. The core takes an injected `isDictionaryWord`; the app passes the bundled word lists (`src/main/vocab/dictionary.ts`) and the vocabulary files to every call view it opens, and again when either changes. The window's page folds raw events with neither, so the app sends it the corrected lines on the call's stream. Given the app's shipped fold options, akou must pass a real dictionary. Test: a vocabulary file entry in a running app, read through the API, the call's stream, the pack, the export and the window; assert `Kubernetes (heard: "kubernetis")`, and that a file pair on a real word (`world`) stays inert while a call-scoped one applies.
- **An unconfirmed entry does something** [decision] (M1). Given `confirmed: false`, the entry must be absent from the decode list and from read-time correction. Test: an unconfirmed entry with a matching heard form; assert no correction and no `vocab.used` entry.
- **An approval that approved nothing answered ok** [VOC-A1] (M2). Given `POST /vocab/approve` for a word proposed into another workspace or call, or misspelt, the word is still unconfirmed, so the answer must name it under `unmatched` (a word already confirmed there counts as found), and `akou_vocab_approve` must be an error, `not_found`, when it approved none of its words, so an agent never tells the user a word was approved; `akou vocab approve` exits 3 and names them. - **The pass corrects a span that is not there** [decision] (M2). Given a post-call pass output whose `heard` span does not occur in the named segment, akou must drop that correction, the same rule as enhanced-note citations. Test: a fake provider returning a bad span.
- **A YAML term turns into a boolean** [design] (M1). Given a term `No`, `On` or `Yes`, the writer must quote every string and the reader must get a string back. Test: round-trip those terms.
- **Biasing measured on synthetic speech only** [design] (M1). Given a claim about hit rates or insertions, it must come from the nightly evaluation that includes the real-call clips, never from the TTS set alone. The TTS numbers guard against regressions and say nothing about accuracy. Test: the evaluation job fails if the real set is missing rather than passing on the synthetic one.

## Kept from closed traps, as invariants

The predecessors fixed these, and they must stay true in akou:

- The mic never waits for the call side and the call side never clocks the mic.
- One continuous resampler per stream, never per chunk.
- akou applies gain only to the recognizer's copy, never to the recording.
- Health monitors do nothing while paused; one monitor tick never runs two rebuilds.
- Recording uses only crash-safe containers (Ogg Opus).
- Tests never play audio through real outputs; fixtures are generated, never real calls.
- Language and quality claims come from real recordings in the nightly evaluation, never from synthetic speech alone.
- A start answers 201 only after audio is being written, and never leaves a call that looks live after a failure.
- The final pass runs after every ending, including endings that happened while akou was not running.
- A stale restart (last audio over an hour old) needs explicit confirmation.

- **Existing-file overwrite policy on start (a collision should have answered 409)** [T1.34]. Retired by F4.0: call folders are unique by construction and nothing is overwritten.
- **Makefile and Xcode version breakage** [T1.46]. Retired by F4.10: there is no Makefile; `bun` and `cargo` are the interface.
- **Comparison-lane tool exited 0 with an error message** [T2.14]. Retired by F2.32: the third-party comparison lane is dropped.
- **Page refetched the whole transcript every second** [T2.50]. Retired by F2.50: the window gets RPC pushes and other clients use SSE with a cursor.
- **Meet userscript never exercised** [T4.16]. Retired by F4.6: there is no userscript; meeting detection, if built, uses OS signals.
